About Me

My Story

tl;dr Denver → sports → geography → Wesleyan University BA 2023 → astrophysics → University of California, Irvine MS 2025 → volunteering → basketball analytics → data science

Hello, hello! I grew up in the lovely state of Denver, Colorado as a lifelong Nuggets fan– spiritually invigorated by Carmelo Anthony, Denver sports, geography, learning languages, film, philosophy, and watching both Manchester City and Sumo Wrestling. Being foundationally moved by the idea of trying to contextualize my place in the Universe, I attended Wesleyan University, graduating with a B.A. degree in 2023 with a double major in Physics and Astronomy and a minor in Data Analysis. My research during my undergraduate career spanned the Universe’s youngest stellar systems (with the Disk Detectives Research Group), using the gas they still fling about them to create some of the most accurate and precise stellar mass measurements ever made, to the Universe’s very oldest galaxies (at the Cosmic Dawn Center, Niels Bohr Institute), using gravitational lensing to study colorful pinwheels ~12.4 billion years in the past.

I pursued this passion further by attaining a M.S. degree in Astrophysics from the University of California, Irvine (UCI) in 2025. At UCI, I advised the SCALES science team at Keck Observatory through simulations of next generation technology, finding the 10 highest priority stellar systems to resolve water around as a Graduate Student Researcher. As a Teaching Assistant of 7 physics and astronomy courses, I directly taught and mentored over 500 students. I also contributed to various graduate student groups at UCI including the Underrepresented geNders In physics & asTronomY group (UNITY), the PhysAstro Blog, the UCI Physics and Astronomy Excellence group (PACE), and I co-founded the UCI Allyship Discussion Group (UADG). Additionally, I completed science outreach at both Wesleyan and UCI to underserved local communities by presenting engaging physics demonstrations and helping host public observing nights at the Van Vleck Observatory.

Since graduating from UCI, I have shifted my focus to AI Image Generation as an AI MOVE Fellow with Handshake, and basketball analytics as a Data Science Intern with CBB Analytics. My recent personal projects (create link!) generally follow my passion for the data science of basketball at various levels: NCAA, WNBA, and NBA.

Extracurricular Interests

Volunteering

I absolutely love volunteering! Besides it being an activity with personal physical and mental health benefits (Mayo Clinic), I think it’s really important to devote some part of your time and energy in the service of others. Previously, I volunteered with Project CURE in Denver, Colorado, which supplies medical items to worldwide communities in need, organizing materials for shipment. Additionally, I spent 2 summers in Tianjin, China as a Recreation Intern serving Shepherd’s Field Children’s Village (now known as the Philip Hayden Foundation). Currently in Sacramento, I volunteer at 2 different food distribution centers: South Sacramento Christian Center (SSCC) and G7 Thy Presence’s School of Faith Ministries. I volunteer on Saturday mornings and Sunday afternoons by unloading and sorting deliveries, organizing food from freezers, refrigerators, and storage, assembling food boxes, bagging human and pet food, directing traffic, operating a pallet jack, and loading food boxes into vehicles. Through these contributions, I strengthened my organizational and teamwork skills. If you want to jump in and understand how you may give back to your community, please do a quick google search– it’s generally easier to find and join opportunities than you’d expect!

Geography

Since high school, geography has consistently been one of my favorite topics to think about and study. I can’t really explain why. National Geographic says: “Geography is the study of places and the relationships between people and their environments. Geographers explore both the physical properties of Earth’s surface and the human societies spread across it. They also examine how human culture interacts with the natural environment and the way that locations and places can have an impact on people. Geography seeks to understand where things are found, why they are there and how they develop and change over time.” I simply adore all of that stuff. Geography is not particularly well-taught in America, yet I think it’s a subject deeply enriching to the human spirit. You inhabit this world (currently), you should definitely know some things about it! During my free time, I play Geoguessr (peak competitive rank in multiplayer duels: 1000; peak division: Master II), read geography and geohistory books, and collect old maps.
