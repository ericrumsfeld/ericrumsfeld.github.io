NBA 2026-27 Standings Predictions

Created on October 20, 2026

2026   ·   work

On the eve of what is slated to be the NBA’s most exciting regular season to date, how do I forecast the teams to finish when all is said and done?
For the first time in a long time, the Eastern Conference projects to be stronger than the West, with LeBron James and Kawhi Leonard heading east, and Giannis Antetokounmpo and Jaylen Brown joining forces with budding superteams.
Will the Knicks experience a championship hangover? Should we doubt the Nuggets as a playoff lock? Which team(s) will surprise us all and take the NBA by storm as the Spurs did last season?
Find out now in my NBA 2026-27 Regular Season standings predictions, with nuanced modeling for off-season moves, roster continuity, team identity, and injury luck.

Western Conference


1. San Antonio Spurs [last: 2 seed, 62-20]

Of course, the Spurs’ success rides on the shoulders of their 7’4 giant, Victor Wembanyama. Posting the 4th best offense and 3rd best defense last season, with fantastic continuity and a clear identity, the Spurs will have another immensely successful season if Wemby can stay healthy. This team is young, always developing, always feisty, and their frontcourt depth has been strongly bolstered through their off-season moves. The franchise has a clear intent to put physicality around Wemby, raising their floor, and Dylan Harper’s seemingly endless potential gives me optimism for a high ceiling. Although each team in the Southwestern Conference should be improved from last year, potentially stealing a few more wins from the Spurs (last year they went 13-3 against division rivals), I think the talent and system here is too great to ultimately deny their top spot in the West.

Notable Departures A+

  • None... yet.

Notable Additions A−

  • Tobias Harris provides a super reliable veteran presence on both ends of the court. Quaintance and Reed Jr. add significant depth at the four/big-man position.

Roster Continuity A+

  • Kept their most important players: Wemby, Fox, Castle, Harper, Champagnie, Johnson, Barnes, Bryant, and Kornet.
  • Lost Olynyk, Sochan, and Biyombo, but these aren't particularly notable departures. Overall, excellent roster continuity.

System & Coaching A+

  • It's easy to win games when you have a 7'4" defensive demigod breaking out as a potential future GOAT candidate. The Wemby-centric system features feisty role players who create disruptions on both ends of the court.
  • Mitch Johnson (CBS Rank: 9) had some potential missteps in last season's playoffs, but winning so many games without Wemby during the regular season gives me confidence that he can correct those mistakes in just his second year in charge.

Injury Projection C

  • Tobias Harris suffered a calf strain during training camp that may keep him out of the season opener. Hopefully, this doesn't linger into the regular season.
  • Quaintance may not join the team on the court until midseason, even under an optimistic projection.
  • One would expect their great injury luck last season to regress toward the mean, potentially creating more challenges than they experienced last year.

7. Dallas Mavericks [last: 12 seed, 26-56]

Most NBA 2026-27 standings predictions seemingly take a swing on the Mavericks, Grizzlies, or Jazz to be much improved from last season. My bet is on the Mavs. I personally am very excited for first time NBA coach Dusty May (CBS Rank 26) who was amazing in college and I think he’s going to bring a fresh, analytically driven approach to this team. Yes, the roster has Masai Ujiri’s fingerprint all over it with an overabundance of unspecialized tall wings, and there are reasons to seriously doubt its 3 point shooting and ball handling depth as a result, but I think Cooper Flagg will truly break out as a star, finishing the season with serious all-NBA consideration.

Notable Departures C-

  • Klay Thompson
  • Ryan Nembhard
  • Brandon Williams

Notable Additions A−

  • Kyrie Irving, played 0 games for them last year rehabbing from ACL tear late in the 2024-25 season
  • Santi Aldama
  • Zaccharie Risacher
  • Marcus Sasser
  • Morez Johnson Jr

Roster Continuity B+

  • The mavs keep the majority of their key contributors and depth, although there are a few notable changes, the replacements should slide right in without too much adjustment.

System & Coaching A+

  • Dusty May (CBS Rank 26) is a new head coach, but will bring a solid, uniting personality to the team and an analytically driven offense centered on foundational principles.

Injury Projection A

  • The Mavs were average to somewhat unlucky last season with injuries. Besides Dereck Lively II, there’s no reason to believe they would become very unlucky this season, so injury factor shouldn’t play a huge role outside of Kyrie’s reintroduction.
